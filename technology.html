Science at the Core of Beauty

Peptide

Peptides are short chains of amino acids, the building blocks of proteins. In the human body, they act as biological messengers that help regulate the functions of the skin, hair and other tissues.

Today, peptides are widely used in modern medicine, including treatments for diabetes and obesity. They are also being actively studied for wound healing, tissue regeneration, inflammation control and the recovery of damaged tissue.

Peptides in Cosmetics

In cosmetics, selected peptides are used to support skin elasticity, hydration and barrier care, as well as a healthy scalp and hair condition. Different peptide structures can provide different functional benefits.

How Skin Absorbs

The outermost layer of skin is its most powerful barrier — and cosmetics' greatest challenge.

Active ingredients penetrate through transdermal absorption, mainly via the Intercellular Route: narrow channels between skin cells, filled with ceramides and other skin lipids — weaving through in a slow, winding zigzag.

Our Pressure Dispensing Applicator is designed to make this possible.
